   `/Users/vampire/Desktop/projects/amzonClone/app/globals.css:103-106`

   Tailwind v4 places component and utility classes in cascade layers, but the new `a { color: inherit; text-decoration: none; }` rule is unlayered. Unlayered normal declarations take precedence over layered declarations, so this rule overrides styles such as:
