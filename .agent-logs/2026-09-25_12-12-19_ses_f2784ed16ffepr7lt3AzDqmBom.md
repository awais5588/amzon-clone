### Reseed safety

The most important compatibility hazard is `/Users/vampire/Desktop/projects/amzonClone/scripts/seed.ts:455-486`:

- It deletes all products and reviews.
- `insertMany` generates new product `_id`s.
- It does not migrate or clear carts.
- It does not clear orders.
- It resets stock to the literal seed quantities.

Consequences:

- Active and saved carts can become unresolvable.
- Order `product` references can dangle, although current order pages still render from snapshots.
- Previously sold stock can be resurrected.
- Historical reviews disappear.
- A SKU change causes old cart lines to be dropped; an SKU reused for a materially different product would corrupt historical meaning.

A replacement migration should preserve `_id`s where practical, explicitly map old SKUs in carts, preserve historical order snapshots, and reconcile stock deliberately. The destructive seed should not be used against a persistent or production-like database.
